## How I think about finance systems

- Start from the standard.  A readiness checklist built from public frameworks (COSO, SOX and PCAOB, COBIT, DAMA), then the GL master as the foundation everything else reads from.
- Certify the queries, not the snapshots.  Prove the data once, and no one witnesses or retypes a balance again.
- No roll-forward.  Each rec is one living file that pulls its own evidence, and every number is backed by a source document.
- Bring the close to the accountant.  The system flags what's missing and proposes the journals, so people spend their time on the high-risk accounts.
- AI prepares, a person signs off.  AI works under its own identity, never under a person's name.
- A control that catches nothing proves nothing.  The checks I build are proven against planted failures.

## How I work with AI

I'm not a coder, and I don't pretend to be. The accountant in me doesn't trust a number until it ties out, and I hold AI output to the same standard.
